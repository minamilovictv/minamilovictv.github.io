Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this website usable by everyone.

The Western Balkans Fund is committed to providing a website that is accessible, inclusive, and usable for everyone, regardless of ability, technology, or circumstance.

We strive to design and maintain our website in accordance with internationally recognized web accessibility standards and continuously work to improve accessibility across all pages and digital services.

Our accessibility efforts include:

  • Clear and consistent navigation
  • Readable typography and appropriate colour contrast
  • Responsive layouts for different devices
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation where possible
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Structured headings and semantic HTML
  • Continuous review of accessibility improvements

Despite our efforts, some content or documents may not yet fully meet current accessibility standards. We are committed to addressing identified issues as resources permit.

If you encounter accessibility barriers or require information in an alternative format, we encourage you to contact the Secretariat. We value your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to improve accessibility for all users.
