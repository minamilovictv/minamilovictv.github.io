Flagship Capacity-Building Programme

Strengthening Civil Society through Regional Learning and European Partnerships

Peer to Peer connects civil society organisations across the Western Balkans with their counterparts in the European Union. Implemented by the Western Balkans Fund and funded by Germany's Federal Foreign Office, the programme brings together 12 organisations from all six Contracting Parties for a full cycle of exchange and capacity building: expert-led training in monitoring and evaluation, strategic communication and countering misinformation, followed by mentoring, study visits with EU peer organisations and local trainings that pass the knowledge on across the region.

Why Peer to Peer

Why the Programme Matters

Civil society organisations across the Western Balkans operate in an increasingly complex environment shaped by evolving funding landscapes, growing public expectations and rapidly changing information ecosystems. Building resilient organisations today requires more than technical expertise. It requires strong governance, effective communication, evidence-based decision making and the ability to respond to misinformation while maintaining public trust.

Peer to Peer gives participating organisations the space to address these challenges together, connecting with peers, field experts and counterparts in the European Union, and taking the time to reflect, reshape and reimpact.

One programme, four connected formats

A Journey of Learning and Collaboration

The Peer to Peer journey combines intensive expert training, one-to-one mentoring and first-hand exchange with European civil society organisations. From the residential training to the EU study visits, each step builds the skills organisations need, knowledge they then carry home and share with peers through their own local trainings.

1. Residential Training

Learning Together

The residential training took place in Belgrade from 14 to 20 June 2026. It was the first part of the programme, bringing together twelve organisations and 24 participants from across the Western Balkans for a full week of collaborative learning and expert-led workshops. Beyond technical knowledge, it helped create lasting professional relationships and encouraged future cross-border cooperation.

2. Online Mentoring

A Tailor-Made Approach

After the residential training in Belgrade, Peer to Peer participants spent the summer putting that learning into practice. Working one-to-one with the programme's three expert trainers, they translated what they had learned into tools built specifically for their own organisations: clear theories of change backed by concrete performance indicators, strategic communication plans tailored to their key stakeholders, and resilience plans to navigate an evolving information landscape.

3. EU Study Visits

Connecting the Western Balkans with Europe

Peer to Peer creates lasting partnerships between civil society organisations from the Western Balkans and their counterparts across the European Union. Through study visits and direct exchange, participants gain first-hand insight into successful organisational models, innovative approaches and sustainable practices while establishing networks that continue beyond the programme itself.

The exchange runs both ways. The visiting organisation gains fresh perspective and practical tools, while the host organisation sees its own practices through a peer's eyes. Beyond the visit itself, it is often the beginning of a lasting partnership.

Programme Impact

Building Stronger Civil Society Across the Region

Peer to Peer strengthens civil society by investing in people, organisations and regional cooperation. Through shared learning, European partnerships and knowledge transfer, the programme contributes to stronger organisational capacities, improved communication, better monitoring and learning practices, increased resilience against misinformation and a more connected civil society community across the Western Balkans.
