About the Programme

People-to-people connections at the heart of regional cooperation

MOVE Grants are the dedicated individual mobility mechanism of the Western Balkans Fund. The programme is built around the individual changemaker: a researcher, journalist, artist, student or activist who wants to cross a border, learn something, collaborate, and bring it back home.

Unlike WBF's larger calls for organisational proposals, MOVE rests on the belief that direct, in-person exchange (academic, artistic, professional) is irreplaceable for building trust across the Western Balkans. A researcher visiting a lab in another country, a journalist attending a regional media forum, an activist participating in a residency: these are the connections that accumulate into something larger.

Each MOVE grantee commits to an agreed structured mobility activity with a formal host institution/organization in another Contracting Party of the Western Balkans Fund. After the visit, they share their experience in their networks, on social media and through WBF's platforms, becoming ambassadors for regional cooperation.

The programme funds short-term, individual mobility within the WB6: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Specific grant amounts, eligibility rules and timing for the next call are being revised; an announcement is expected in autumn 2026.

* This designation is without prejudice to positions on status, and is in line with UNSCR 1244/1999 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo Declaration of Independence.

Stories from past calls

What MOVE looks like in practice

Behind every grant is an individual who used a short window of time to build a connection that would not otherwise have happened. Here are six MOVE grantees from past calls and what they did with their mobility.

SD Skopje · North Macedonia → Sarajevo · Bosnia and Herzegovina
Education & Scientific Exchange

Saša Dimitrijević

Wheelchair basketball beyond borders. Study visits, international championships and a scientific conference across Bosnia and Herzegovina and Serbia strengthened his work in coaching, sports management, referee training and inclusion for persons with disabilities.

I gained valuable knowledge, built meaningful regional partnerships, and returned home inspired to make wheelchair basketball more inclusive and accessible for everyone.
MĐ Podgorica · Montenegro → Belgrade · Serbia
Education & Scientific Exchange

Marija Đurđević Ilić

Music without borders. A series of educational seminars and a solo concert in Serbia, with masterclasses and hands-on training for students and teachers at the Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts and partner music schools.

Music knows no borders — it connects people, inspires learning, and builds lasting regional friendships through the universal language of art.
LG Tirana · Albania → Podgorica · Montenegro
Education & Scientific Exchange

Lorena Gjana

A Western Balkans gaming community. Research visits, industry conferences and strategy meetings with regional gaming associations — 3 Hills Game Studio in Podgorica, the Macedonian Games Industry and the Serbian Game Association — laying the groundwork for a regional incubator and joint projects.

This experience proved that regional cooperation is the key to unlocking innovation, building stronger partnerships, and creating new opportunities for the gaming community across the Western Balkans.
EK Pristina · Kosovo* → Bar · Montenegro
Education & Scientific Exchange

Elza Krelani

Robotic Competition Event. She joined the "Montenegro Makers" robotics competition in Bar, gaining hands-on experience in organising and running robotics events and exchanging best practices with regional experts — groundwork for launching Kosovo's first "Kosovo Makers" competition.

This experience showed me that by learning from our neighbours, we can inspire innovation, empower young people, and build a stronger regional robotics community together.
TT Sarajevo · Bosnia and Herzegovina → Belgrade · Serbia
Sustainable Development

Tamara Teodorović

Magbago — a masterclass at Belgrade's Festival of Alternative Fashion. She led a workshop and showcased products developed from agricultural waste, building partnerships with designers, researchers and sustainable-fashion brands around textile innovation and circular-economy practices.

This mobility experience showed me that regional cooperation is not just about exchanging ideas — it is about building lasting partnerships that inspire innovation and sustainable change.
SŽ Belgrade · Serbia → Banja Luka · Bosnia and Herzegovina & Skopje · North Macedonia
Sustainable Development

Stefan Živković

Strengthening the rare-disease alliances of the Western Balkans. Mobility visits and 13 stakeholder interviews connected rare-disease alliances and patient organisations in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia and North Macedonia, sharing good practices on access to therapies, patient advocacy and organisational sustainability.

By connecting rare disease organizations across the Western Balkans, we can turn shared challenges into shared solutions — building a stronger, united voice for rare disease communities.

Areas of Intervention

Three areas. Many forms of mobility.

MOVE supports mobility initiatives in three broad areas of intervention. These have been consistent across past calls and are expected to remain the orientation of the programme going forward.

01
Cultural Cooperation
Enhancing regional cultural identity and intercultural dialogue through exchange, heritage preservation and mutual understanding.
  • Regional festivals & exhibitions
  • Joint artistic productions
  • Artist residencies
  • Heritage preservation
  • Intercultural dialogue workshops
  • Digital cultural platforms
02
Education & Scientific Exchange
Supporting capacity building, academic and professional mobility, joint research and scientific collaboration across the WB6.
  • University & lab exchanges
  • Workshops & seminars
  • Internships & placements
  • Conferences & symposiums
  • Joint research projects
  • Field research & data collection
03
Sustainable Development
Advancing regional cohesion through partnerships on sustainable economic development, environmental protection and social inclusion.
  • Environmental research
  • Sustainability training
  • Green economy study visits
  • Social inclusion programmes
  • Policy dialogues
  • Collaborative advocacy
