About the Programme

People-to-people links at the heart of regional cooperation

MOVE Grants is a dedicated grant-making mechanism designed to support mobility, exchange, and people-to-people cooperation across the region. The scheme is built on WBF's belief that direct, in-person interactions whether through academic exchange, artistic collaboration, and/or professional mobility are vital to building trust, understanding, and shared values across the Western Balkans.

Each MOVE grantee commits to an agreed structured mobility activity with a formal host institution/organisation in another Contracting Party of the Western Balkans Fund. After the visit, they share their experience within their networks, on social media and through WBF's platforms, becoming ambassadors for regional cooperation.

The programme funds short-term, individual mobility within the WB6: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Full eligibility rules, grant amounts and the submission deadline for Call 7 are set out in the Guidelines for Applicants and in the call package on OGMS.

*This designation is without prejudice to positions on status and is in line with UNSCR 1244 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo declaration of independence.

Before you apply

How a MOVE mobility has to be built

MOVE funds an individual travelling to a host institution in another Contracting Party. A few structural rules decide whether an application is eligible at all, and they are checked before anything else is read.

Mandatory

Every mobility starts with a written invitation

A MOVE application has to rest on a formal partnership with a host institution in another Contracting Party. The partnership is what confirms your mobility is grounded in a structured, mutually agreed framework rather than a self-organised trip.

You prove it with an official invitation letter from the host institution, stating the dates, the programme and the reason you are being invited. A confirmed registration, acceptance or enrolment letter for a course, programme or workshop they organise works too. Without this document the application is rejected at the eligibility check.

At least two Contracting Parties

Your mobility must connect your own Contracting Party with the one where the host institution is based. A single-CP action does not qualify.

Not inside your own place of residence

Mobility that takes place where you already live is not supported, regardless of the activity or the host.

Primarily within the Western Balkans

Activities should take place in the region. In exceptional, justified cases activities in the European Union are eligible where they promote the visibility of the Western Balkans internationally.

Eligible costs

  • Public transport to and from the host location (flights, trains, buses, shuttles, taxis)
  • Personal car, tolls and parking, where directly related to the mobility
  • Medical and travel insurance for the duration of the stay
  • Visa fees for regional mobility
  • Accommodation, at local market rates and against fiscal invoices
  • Daily allowance for activity and travel days
  • Special-needs support for applicants with disabilities
  • Registration or participation fees for conferences, training, workshops and residencies
  • Visibility costs for promoting the mobility action
  • Postal and courier costs for documents required by the mobility

Not eligible

  • Salaries, fees and consultancy or external expertise payments
  • Indirect and overhead costs
  • Subcontracting
  • Costs incurred before or after the contractual period, except confirmed registration payments
  • Double funding, meaning anything already financed from another source
  • Personal expenses unrelated to the mobility
  • Entertainment and leisure costs
  • Activities affiliated with political or religious agendas
  • Activities intended for profit-making
  • Debts, interest, currency exchange losses, fines and legal charges
