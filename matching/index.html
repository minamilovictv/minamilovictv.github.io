The Matching Grant Scheme

Supporting regional cooperation across the Western Balkans

The Western Balkans Fund's Matching Grants scheme supports small, short-term regional cooperation projects across the six WB6 Contracting Parties: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Through this mechanism, the Fund co-finances initiatives led by civil society organisations and other non-profit entities, helping them grow local project ideas into genuinely regional, cross-border collaborations.

Grants do not exceed €10,000 and cover up to half of a project's total eligible costs, with the remaining share required to come from another donor rather than the applicant's own resources. Funded projects run for two to four months (including the final reporting phase) and take place within the Western Balkans region itself, with only occasional exceptions for activities that promote the region on an international stage.

*This designation is without prejudice to positions on status and is in line with UNSCR 1244 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo declaration of independence.

Priority areas

Where matching grants make a difference

An eligible project must fall under at least one of the three priority areas below.

01
Cultural cooperation

Bringing communities together through shared cultural practice and creative exchange across borders.

  • Regional festivals
  • Heritage preservation
  • Artistic exchanges
02
Education & scientific exchange

Advancing knowledge, skills and joint learning between institutions and communities in the region.

  • Academic events
  • Regional research
  • Capacity building
03
Sustainable development

Building a greener, more resilient regional economy that creates opportunity for young people.

  • Youth entrepreneurship
  • Tourism
  • Green economy
  • SME support

Cross-cutting themes are rewarded. Gender, youth, inclusion, environment and EU integration are encouraged throughout, and scored during the evaluation of every proposal.

Who can apply

Eligible applicants & partners

Applicants and partners must be legal, non-profit entities based in the WB6, with prior experience in donor-funded or regional projects.

CSOs & NGOs
Public bodies
Educational institutions
Business associations
Media associations
Cultural & sports institutions

Every applicant must

  • Be a legal, non-profit entity based in one of the WB6 Contracting Parties.
  • Have prior experience managing donor-funded or regional projects.
  • Be able to demonstrate secured co-funding for the remaining share.
  • Not be currently funded or rejected under another WBF grant.
What counts as an eligible project

What makes a strong application

Regional cooperation sits at the heart of every application: projects must bring together partners from several WBF Contracting Parties, reflecting an insistence on substantive rather than symbolic collaboration. A competitive project meets all four criteria below.

Genuine regional partnership

Involves partners from at least two WBF Contracting Parties, with activities inside the WB6 region.

Clear, regional results

Is regionally relevant and shows concrete, measurable results in at least one priority area.

Ready to move fast

Can be implemented rapidly, within a project window of two to four months.

A real visibility plan

Includes a clear plan to acknowledge and communicate WBF's support throughout.

Not covered: salaries & human resources, external expertise & consultancy, indirect costs, any expenditure incurred before the contract date, and anything of a political, religious, or luxury nature.
