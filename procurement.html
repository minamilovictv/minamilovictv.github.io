How to submit an offer

Each notice above links to its own dossier, which sets out the technical specification, the evaluation criteria and the documents an offer must contain. Where the dossier and this page differ, the dossier governs.

Offers received after the stated deadline are not evaluated. Deadlines are given in Central European Time.

Questions about a notice
info@westernbalkansfund.org
  1. 1Download the dossier for the notice you are bidding on and read the eligibility and evaluation criteria in full.
  2. 2Send any clarification questions to the address opposite, quoting the reference number, well before the deadline.
  3. 3Prepare your offer in the format the dossier requires, including every supporting document it lists.
  4. 4Submit before the deadline shown on the notice, by the method the dossier specifies.
