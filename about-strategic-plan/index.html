Adopted Strategy · 2026–2030

Supporting the Champions of Regional Cooperation

The Western Balkans Fund's second Strategic Plan, a refreshed, five-year roadmap that moves beyond grant-making alone to a more comprehensive, integrated and inclusive way of backing the people who make regional cooperation happen.

Explore the Strategy View Programmes
The Vision

A region at peace with itself, integrated into its European home

The Western Balkans Fund envisions a region where exchange and mutually beneficial cooperation between people, at all levels and from all walks of life, is the norm rather than the exception. Built with WBF leadership and the Secretariat, and shaped by the Council of Senior Officials of the six Contracting Parties, this plan is ambitious yet grounded, rolling out through a carefully phased programme of investment.

Programme Mission
“The WBF promotes ever closer cooperation and stronger ties between the Contracting Parties, their integration into the European Union, and a common presentation within and beyond the region, supporting the champions of regional cooperation through a diversified programme of grants, dialogue and technical assistance.”
— WBF Strategic Plan 2026–2030
Theory of Change

From direct support to lasting regional change

By investing directly in regional initiatives and the champions who drive them, the Fund improves neighbourly relations that transcend geographical and cultural boundaries, and normalises cooperation as an essential part of a stable, prosperous, European future.

What Five Years Taught Us

Lessons that shaped the refreshed strategy

Insights from the Fund's first strategic period point to a more integrated, partnership-driven way of supporting champions of regional cooperation.

01
Integration delivers better value
Grants achieve far more when paired with tailored technical support, mentoring and coaching, strengthening both projects and the organisations behind them.
02
Partnership is a continuous win-win
Working with complementary agencies lets the Fund offer a more holistic package of support and raises visibility for grantees and the WBF alike.
03
Convening multiplies impact
Bringing grantees together to celebrate, learn and network sustains the value of the Fund's investment well beyond a single project's lifecycle.
04
Longer-term funding drives real change
Measurable impact is best achieved through extended relationships where impact is designed into both the project and the ongoing technical support.
05
Champions are everywhere
Small, grassroots and rural organisations can be as committed as large city groups. The Fund keeps its promise to these "hard-to-fund" champions.
The Plan in a Nutshell

Three strategic outcomes

Funding for civil society remains central, now complemented by two upgraded components that make the Fund a more dynamic, effective and visible catalyst for cooperation across the region.

Outcome 1

Grant-making & Technical Support

Flexible grants and targeted technical assistance packages focused on measurable value, more agile, less bureaucratic, and adapted to an evolving regional context.

  • Strategic partnerships for complementary skills
  • A wider range of grants for a wider range of grantees
  • Support based on organisational needs assessments
Outcome 2

Convening & Advocacy

Regular, frequent opportunities for civil society champions across the region to meet, dialogue, share, advocate and learn, with interactive, skilfully facilitated events in the Contracting Parties.

  • Recognised as a go-to convener by 2028
  • A dedicated Convening, Communications & Advocacy section
  • Learning workshops on critical cooperation issues
Outcome 3

Information Exchange Hub

A Knowledge Exchange Platform, evolving from today's Partnership Platform, as an accessible home for information and learning about funding, partnership opportunities and networking.

  • Platform launched & publicly accessible
  • A curated space for blogs, insights & resources
  • Actively maintained and integrated into WBF services
A Refreshed Grants Package

A more flexible way to fund cooperation

Five granting instruments, all aligned with the Theory of Change and all prioritising multi-Contracting-Party cooperation as a core principle.

Tiered core funding
Project & Thematic Grants
Grants for localised projects with regional impact and broader multilateral cooperation. Small, medium and large tiers give flexibility across project scopes, while matching and co-financing mechanisms jointly support impactful regional initiatives.
SmallMediumLargeMatching & co-financing
Institutional strengthening
Capacity Development & Technical Assistance
Tailored support that builds the operational capacity CSOs need to engage in effective regional cooperation. It addresses the complexities of multi-Contracting-Party projects and the organisational resilience required to lead and sustain regional initiatives over the long term.
Rapid response
Responsive Grants for Emerging Regional Needs
Nimble, flexible funding that enables swift responses to evolving regional challenges. Adaptable to emerging sectoral priorities and specific circumstances, these grants facilitate regional teamwork within tight timeframes and foster timely solutions across the Western Balkans.
Learning & exchange
Scholarships & Fellowships
Academic and research scholarships for students and researchers focused on Western Balkans topics, plus leadership fellowships for civil society professionals. Together they scale up learning and experience-sharing, a core part of normalising cooperation through frequent exchange.
Academic & researchCivil society leaders
Long-term partnership
Framework & Partnership Grants
Multi-year grants for larger organisations and consortia working towards long-term strategic goals such as gender equality, EU integration or regional stability, sustaining momentum, enhancing visibility and ensuring the scalability of successful regional initiatives.
Multi-yearConsortia
Underpinning all five instruments

One consistent rulebook. A comprehensive Standard Operating Procedures manual will define every grant mechanism, giving grantees clarity and the Fund consistency across all instruments.

Phased Roadmap

Implementation timeline

A measured, sustainable trajectory, consolidating the current portfolio first, then progressively diversifying, with a mid-term review in 2028.

2026
Foundation
Action Plan & Partnership Platform
Secure strategic partnerships, launch the Partnership Platform, endorse the fundraising framework, roll out new staff ToRs with a Management by Objectives system, and recruit a Deputy Executive Director.
2027
Restructure
Decentralised management
Reorganise the Secretariat around the programme portfolio, establish a Resource & Partnership Development Unit, strengthen the MEL Unit, and integrate grant ceremonies into convening events.
2028
Evaluate
Convening section & mid-term review
Stand up the Convening, Communications & Advocacy section, diversify the funding portfolio, and commission an external evaluation alongside a mid-term strategy review.
2029
Deepen
A wider grant portfolio
Offer a broader range of grants to more grantees, deliver technical assistance packages built on organisational needs assessments, and expand the MEL Unit's mandate.
2030
Renew
Next strategic cycle
Conduct a full context analysis and stakeholder needs review, ensuring the next strategic cycle is built on the most current and relevant evidence.
