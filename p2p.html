Flagship Capacity-Building Programme

Strengthening Civil Society through Regional Learning and European Partnerships

Peer to Peer is a regional capacity-building programme implemented by the Western Balkans Fund to strengthen civil society organisations across the Western Balkans. Through expert-led learning, peer exchange, study visits to European organisations and local knowledge sharing, the programme equips civil society leaders with practical skills, stronger partnerships and new approaches to creating sustainable impact.

Why Peer to Peer

Why the Programme Matters

Civil society organisations across the Western Balkans operate in an increasingly complex environment shaped by evolving funding landscapes, growing public expectations and rapidly changing information ecosystems. Building resilient organisations today requires more than technical expertise. It requires strong governance, effective communication, evidence-based decision making and the ability to respond to misinformation while maintaining public trust.

Peer to Peer was created to address these challenges by connecting organisations across the region with experienced European partners, creating opportunities to learn from one another and strengthen the civil society sector together.

How the Programme Works

A Journey of Learning and Collaboration

1

Residential Training

An intensive residential learning experience combines expert sessions, practical workshops and peer exchange focused on today's most important organisational challenges.

2

Online Mentoring

Expert mentoring supports participants as they transform new knowledge into practical action within their own organisations.

3

Study Visits

Participants visit experienced European civil society organisations to exchange knowledge, observe good practices and establish long-term partnerships.

4

Local Knowledge Sharing

Participants deliver local training activities that transfer knowledge to their national civil society communities, multiplying the programme's impact across the region.

Programme Focus

Core Learning Areas

01

Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning

Develop practical systems for measuring results, improving organisational performance and demonstrating long-term impact.

02

Strategic Communication

Strengthen organisational storytelling, public engagement and communication strategies that build trust and visibility.

03

Fighting Misinformation

Develop practical skills for identifying misinformation, strengthening information resilience and promoting evidence-based public communication.

Residential Training

Learning Together

The residential training represents the first part of the programme, bringing together participants from across the Western Balkans for several days of collaborative learning, expert-led workshops and regional networking. Beyond technical knowledge, it creates lasting professional relationships and encourages future cross-border cooperation.

European Partnerships

Connecting the Western Balkans with Europe

Peer to Peer creates lasting partnerships between civil society organisations from the Western Balkans and their counterparts across the European Union. Through study visits and direct exchange, participants gain first-hand insight into successful organisational models, innovative approaches and sustainable practices while establishing networks that continue beyond the programme itself.

Programme Impact

Building Stronger Civil Society Across the Region

Peer to Peer strengthens civil society by investing in people, organisations and regional cooperation. Through shared learning, European partnerships and knowledge transfer, the programme contributes to stronger organisational capacities, improved communication, better monitoring and learning practices, increased resilience against misinformation and a more connected civil society community across the Western Balkans.
