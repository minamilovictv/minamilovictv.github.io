The Fund

Who we are

The Western Balkans Fund (WBF) is an intergovernmental organisation based in Tirana, Albania, established by the governments of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Conceived as a fully regionally owned and all-inclusive initiative, the Fund stands as a tangible expression of a renewed spirit of cooperation in the Western Balkans, a mechanism created by the region, for the region, to bring its people, institutions and communities closer together.

WBF is the first home-grown regional mechanism that boost cooperation through grants for civil society organisations and other stakeholders in the Western Balkans Six. Its founding reflects a shared conviction that lasting reconciliation and a common European future are built not only between governments, but between citizens, civil-society organisations and communities working side by side on concrete, cross-border projects.

*This designation is without prejudice to positions on status and is in line with UNSCR 1244 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo Declaration of Independence.

History

WBF establishment

The Agreement for the creation of the Western Balkans Fund was signed by the Ministers of Foreign Affairs of the Western Balkans Six in November 2015.

Following the completion of ratification procedures in all six national parliaments, the Fund became operational on 1 October 2017.

The Fund is financed by its six Contracting Parties in equal quotas, ensuring balanced ownership and shared responsibility across the region. Its founding Executive Director, elected by the WBF Conference of Ministers of Foreign Affairs, is Mr Gjergj Murra, a career diplomat from Albania, who continues to lead the organisation. The Secretariat, seated in Tirana, brings together experts from across the Western Balkans, reflecting the regional character of the institution at every level.

Governance is exercised through the Conference of Ministers of Foreign Affairs as the Fund's highest body, supported by the Council of Senior Officials and the Secretariat. The presidency of the Fund rotates annually among the Contracting Parties; in 2026 it is held by Montenegro.

History

Key Milestones

From the founding agreement in 2015 to a new generation of programmes — a decade of building regional cooperation across the Western Balkans.

  1. November 2015

    Establishment of the Western Balkans Fund

    The Foreign Ministers of the Western Balkans Six signed the Agreement establishing the Western Balkans Fund, creating the region’s first regionally owned international organization dedicated to fostering regional cooperation, trust, and reconciliation.

  2. October 2017

    WBF Becomes Operational

    Following ratification by all six Contracting Parties, the WBF Secretariat officially opened in Tirana, Albania, marking the beginning of the Fund’s operational activities.

  3. November 2017

    First Regional Grant Programme Launched

    WBF launched its first Call for Proposals, providing financial support to regional civil society initiatives and laying the foundation for a growing portfolio of projects connecting people and institutions across the Western Balkans.

  4. December 2020

    First Strategic Development Plan (2020–2024) Adopted

    The Fund adopted its first multi-year Strategic Development Plan, establishing a long-term vision for institutional development, programme growth, and regional impact.

  5. 2021

    European Union Becomes a Strategic Partner

    The European Union and WBF launched their first Joint Action under the IPA programme, significantly expanding the Fund’s grant-making capacity and enabling greater support for regional cooperation initiatives.

  6. 2021

    Switzerland Becomes a Strategic Partner

    The Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation partnered with WBF, strengthening the institution and supporting the development of innovative programmes focused on mobility, dialogue, and regional exchange.

  7. March 2023

    WBF MOVE Grants Programme Launched

    WBF introduced its first individual mobility programme, creating new opportunities for citizens across the Western Balkans to exchange knowledge, develop professionally, and strengthen regional cooperation.

  8. May 2024

    Empowering Regional Cooperation (ERC) Programme Launched

    WBF launched its largest grant programme to date, increasing the maximum grant size and expanding support for ambitious regional cooperation initiatives with greater long-term impact.

  9. June 2024

    Western Balkans–Visegrad Fellowship Launched

    In partnership with the International Visegrad Fund, WBF established its first international fellowship programme, opening new opportunities for academic exchange and cooperation between the Western Balkans and the Visegrad region.

  10. January 2026

    Second Strategic Development Plan (2026–2030) Adopted

    Building on the achievements of its first decade, WBF adopted its second Strategic Development Plan (2026–2030), setting the Fund’s vision for the next phase of institutional growth, programme innovation, strategic partnerships, and regional impact.

  11. May 2026

    Gender Equality Fund Launched

    Co-funded by the European Union and Switzerland, WBF launched the Gender Equality Fund, its first dedicated thematic grant programme and the largest funding scheme in its history, providing grants of up to €40,000 to advance gender equality across the Western Balkans.

  12. September 2026

    WBF Regional Academy Launched

    WBF Regional Academy is a flagship leadership programme that brings together emerging leaders from across the region to strengthen dialogue, cooperation, and a lasting culture of regional collaboration, with support from the Open Society Foundations Western Balkans.

Our Mission

The Western Balkans Fund is dedicated to promoting reconciliation, strengthening regional cooperation, and fostering closer ties among the Contracting Parties of the Western Balkans. Through its initiatives, the Fund contributes to building trust, dialogue, and sustainable partnerships across the region.

Values

The Western Balkans Fund operates in full alignment with the vision of its founding members, supporting the principles of reconciliation, regional solidarity, and the shared European future of all Contracting Parties. Our work is guided by cooperation, inclusiveness, transparency, and mutual respect.

Objectives

What we work toward

WBF promotes cooperation and common values among citizens, civil society and institutions by funding small and medium-sized regional projects and by enabling people-to-people contacts across borders. Its support is concentrated in three core areas of intervention: cultural cooperation, education and scientific exchange, and sustainable development, with youth engagement, gender equality, media development, environmental protection and European integration mainstreamed as cross-cutting priorities.

Over the years, the Fund has grown from a single annual grant scheme into a diversified portfolio that today serves organisations, grassroots actors and individuals alike. This portfolio includes the Grant for Grassroots Initiatives (the Fund's flagship annual call), the Empowering Regional Cooperation Grants, the Gender Equality Fund, the WBF Move Grants for individual mobility, the Western Balkans–Visegrad Fellowship, the WBF Matching Grants, and the WBF Regional Academy on dialogue and a culture of cooperation. Alongside its grant-making, WBF convenes major regional conferences and dialogue initiatives that address shared challenges, from countering hate speech and disinformation to advancing women's leadership and gender equality.

Partners

Working alongside the region's key institutions

The Fund's growth has been made possible by a widening circle of trusted partners and donors who share its commitment to a stable, connected and prosperous Western Balkans.

The European Union is the Fund's principal partner, co-funding its flagship grant schemes since 2021 through the Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance (IPA) under a dedicated EU–WBF Joint Action.

The Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation has supported the Fund's institutional strengthening and portfolio diversification since 2021, with the partnership renewed for the period 2026–2029.

The Government of Japan, through its Ministry of Foreign Affairs, has been a key partner in the Fund's advocacy agenda, enabling its flagship regional conferences on issues such as hate speech, women's leadership and disinformation. The International Visegrad Fund supports the Fund's Western Balkans–Visegrad Fellowship and contributes to its broader institutional work, including the internship programme. In 2026 the Federal Republic of Germany signed its first Cooperation Agreement with the Fund. The Open Society Foundations – Western Balkans (OSF-WB) supported the WBF Regional Academy.

Beyond its donors, the Fund works closely with regional and international institutions, including the Regional Cooperation Council, the Regional Youth Cooperation Office and others, to enhance the visibility, effectiveness and impact of regional cooperation. These partnerships are built on the principles of complementarity, transparency, responsiveness and mutual respect.
