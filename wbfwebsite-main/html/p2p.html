Peer to Peer

Learning exchanges that turn a network of grantees into a community of practice.

The Peer to Peer programme facilitates structured learning exchanges between WBF grantees and partner organisations across the six Contracting Parties — sharing practice, building skills, and strengthening the regional civil society network.

Programme type
Capacity building
Who it serves
Grantees & partner CSOs
Region

Exchange formats

Peer mentoring pairs

An experienced organisation is matched with a growing one for sustained one-to-one guidance.

Thematic learning circles

Small groups of organisations working on similar themes meet to exchange methods and results.

Cross-organisation shadowing

Staff spend time inside a peer organisation in another Contracting Party, learning by doing.

Why it exists

Peer to Peer is designed to complement WBF grant-making with long-term capacity development and network building — so the connections created through funded projects continue to grow after the grants end.
