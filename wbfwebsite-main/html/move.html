About the Programme

People-to-people connections at the heart of regional cooperation

MOVE Grants are the dedicated individual mobility mechanism of the Western Balkans Fund. The programme is built around the individual changemaker: a researcher, journalist, artist, student or activist who wants to cross a border, learn something, collaborate, and bring it back home.

Unlike WBF's larger calls for organisational proposals, MOVE rests on the belief that direct, in-person exchange (academic, artistic, professional) is irreplaceable for building trust across the Western Balkans. A researcher visiting a lab in another country, a journalist attending a regional media forum, an activist participating in a residency: these are the connections that accumulate into something larger.

Each MOVE grantee commits to an agreed structured mobility activity with a formal host institution/organization in another Contracting Party of the Western Balkans Fund. After the visit, they share their experience in their networks, on social media and through WBF's platforms, becoming ambassadors for regional cooperation.

The programme funds short-term, individual mobility within the WB6: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Specific grant amounts, eligibility rules and timing for the next call are being revised; an announcement is expected in autumn 2026.

* This designation is without prejudice to positions on status, and is in line with UNSCR 1244/1999 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo Declaration of Independence.

Stories from past calls

What MOVE looks like in practice

Behind every grant is an individual who used a short window of time to build a connection that would not otherwise have happened. Here are four MOVE grantees from past calls and what they did with their mobility.

AK Belgrade · Serbia → Pristina · Kosovo*
Cultural Cooperation

Ana K.

Artist residency in textile heritage. A two-week residency with a women-led collective documenting traditional weaving practices, culminating in a joint exhibition piece shown in both cities.

I came to learn one craft and left with a network across two cities. The exhibition was the smallest part of what I brought back.
EB Tirana · Albania → Sarajevo · Bosnia and Herzegovina
Education & Scientific Exchange

Erion B.

Joint research on regional youth migration. Three weeks at a sociology research centre, co-authoring a working paper on intra-Balkan youth mobility patterns that was later published in a regional journal.

Reading the data on regional migration is one thing. Interviewing families across a border is another. The paper looks completely different now.
MP Podgorica · Montenegro → Skopje · North Macedonia
Sustainable Development

Milica P.

Field exchange with an environmental CSO. Ten days embedded with a small organisation working on river-monitoring methodologies, returning to launch a parallel pilot on the Morača basin.

Methods I would have spent a year reading about, I learned in ten days of fieldwork. The pilot at home started the week I came back.
VH Pristina · Kosovo* → Skopje · North Macedonia
Cultural Cooperation

Vlora H.

Documentary photography exchange. A collaborative documentary project on shared culinary heritage across the Western Balkans, exhibited at a regional cultural festival the following spring.

Every conversation across a border ended in food. That is where the project found itself, and where I think it will keep going.

Areas of Intervention

Three areas. Many forms of mobility.

MOVE supports mobility initiatives in three broad areas of intervention. These have been consistent across past calls and are expected to remain the orientation of the programme going forward.

Cultural Cooperation
Enhancing regional cultural identity and intercultural dialogue through exchange, heritage preservation and mutual understanding.
  • Regional cultural festivals, exhibitions and performances
  • Joint artistic productions in theatre, film, music and visual arts
  • Artist residencies and immersive creative programmes
  • Cultural heritage preservation and documentation projects
  • Intercultural dialogue workshops and youth forums
  • Digital cultural platforms and multimedia projects
Education & Scientific Exchange
Supporting capacity building, academic and professional mobility, joint research and scientific collaboration across the WB6.
  • Study and research exchanges at universities and laboratories
  • Workshops, seminars and professional development programmes
  • Academic and professional internships or work placements
  • Conferences, symposiums and academic presentations
  • Joint research projects and scientific publications
  • Field research and data collection across the region
Sustainable Development
Advancing regional cohesion through partnerships on sustainable economic development, environmental protection and social inclusion.
  • Environmental research on climate, biodiversity and resources
  • Sustainability training, workshops and capacity-building
  • Study visits to green economy organisations
  • Social inclusion programmes for marginalised communities
  • Policy dialogues on sustainable development
  • Collaborative advocacy projects for sustainable practices

Terms of the previous call

The conditions below applied to the sixth MOVE call, which closed in November 2025. They are preserved here so previous applicants and reviewers can refer back to them.

Do not plan a budget or application against these figures. Grant amounts, eligible costs, evaluation criteria and timing for the next call are being revised. The next call (Call 7) is expected to open in autumn 2026 with updated conditions, which will be published in a new application package once finalised.

Who could apply (Call 6)

  • Individuals only, aged 18 or over on the application deadline
  • Citizens of one of the WB6 Contracting Parties
  • Established professionals, employed or affiliated individuals, or currently enrolled students
  • No criminal record; good command of English
  • Mobility crossing into at least one other WB6 Contracting Party
  • No active MOVE Grant held at the time of application
  • Formal partnership with a host institution in the destination Contracting Party required

Grant figures (Call 6)

Minimum grant
€500
Maximum grant
€3,000
WBF co-financing
Up to 100%
Disbursement
70% / 30%

What Call 6 covered

  • Travel: flights, trains, buses, local transport. Personal car at no more than €0.30/km via Michelin.
  • Accommodation: up to €65/night within the WB region, €80/night outside.
  • Daily allowance: €40/day flat rate for meals and incidentals.
  • Registration fees: conferences, workshops, residencies, courses.
  • Health insurance & visa costs where applicable.
  • Visibility: up to 7% of the total budget.
  • Excluded: salaries, indirect costs, double-funded items, personal expenses, costs before contract signature.

How Call 6 was evaluated

  • Four-step process: administrative check, technical scoring, Evaluation Panel review, document verification.
  • Scoring grid of 100 points across three sections: project description and coherence (25 pts), regional relevance and call objective (50 pts), operational (25 pts).
  • Minimum threshold of 55 points to be recommended to the Evaluation Panel.
  • Shortlist and reserve list endorsed by the WBF Council of Senior Officials.
  • Provisionally selected applicants submitted ID, criminal record clearance, host invitation and partnership agreement at verification stage.
