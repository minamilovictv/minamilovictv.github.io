Regional Dialogue Sessions

Bringing the region around one table

Regional Dialogue Sessions are the Western Balkans Fund's convening format for structured, cross-border conversation. Each session gathers participants from all six Contracting Parties to work through a shared regional question and leave with concrete next steps.

The Format

What a Regional Dialogue Session is

A Regional Dialogue Session is a facilitated working meeting rather than a conference. Participants from across the Western Balkans are brought together around a single theme, given room to speak candidly, and asked to produce something usable by the end of the day — a shared reading of the problem, a set of recommendations, or a commitment to work together on what comes next.

The format is deliberately small and deliberately mixed. Civil society organisations sit alongside public institutions and academia, so that the conversation reflects the full picture rather than a single perspective.

Why It Matters

Dialogue that produces something

01

Space for honest conversation

Sessions are held under clear ground rules so participants can raise difficult questions without turning them into public positions.

02

All six Contracting Parties at the table

Every session is designed so that no Contracting Party is discussed in its absence, and so that participants leave with counterparts they can call.

03

A written outcome, every time

Each session closes with an agreed record — conclusions, recommendations or follow-up commitments — that participants can carry back to their own institutions.

How It Works

How a session runs

1

Setting the theme

The Fund identifies a question that matters across the region and frames it with input from partners in each Contracting Party.

2

Bringing the right people

Participants are invited to give the table a genuine mix of civil society, public institutions and academic expertise.

3

The session itself

A facilitated working day of short framing inputs and structured discussion, built around working through the theme rather than presenting on it.

4

What follows

Conclusions are written up and shared with participants, and the connections made in the room feed into the Fund's wider programmes.

Themes we convene around

Around the Table

Who takes part

Civil society organisations

Practitioners working on the theme in their own Contracting Party, including current and former WBF grantees.

Public institutions

Representatives of ministries, agencies and local authorities whose mandates touch the question under discussion.

Academia and experts

Researchers and independent experts who bring evidence into the room and help test what is being proposed.
