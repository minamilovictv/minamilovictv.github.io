Our Programs

Programs & Initiatives

The Western Balkans Fund supports regional cooperation through grant programmes, capacity-building initiatives, and policy dialogue. By connecting civil society, public institutions, academia, and other stakeholders across all six Contracting Parties, the Fund fosters lasting partnerships, strengthens regional networks, and promotes good neighbourly relations.

What We Offer

Three ways we back regional cooperation

The Western Balkans Fund works across all six Contracting Parties to turn shared ideas into shared results. Our support falls into three connected areas, each designed to help partners meet, learn, and build something that outlasts a single project. Each one is a column below, with its programmes listed underneath.

Funding Opportunities

From flagship regional schemes to fast, people-to-people mobility funding, our grants back partnerships of every size and ambition across the Western Balkans.

Grants for non-profit organizations

Grants for Grassroots Initiatives (GGI)

GGI supports young, emerging CSOs and Grassroots organizations to participate and contribute to regional cooperation, peacebuilding, good neighborly relations, reconciliation, democratic governance and EU integration across the WB6.

Gender Equality Fund (GEFund)

GeFund is the Western Balkans Fund's dedicated regional funding mechanism for gender equality. By supporting collaborative initiatives across the six Contracting Parties, it transforms the Fund's long-term commitment to women's empowerment into concrete action and regional impact.

Grants for individuals

MOVE Grants

Short term, people to people funding for Researchers, Students, Professors, Journalists, Doctors, Actors, Artists, Musicians, Activists, to cross-border cooperation, connect with peers, and bring knowledge and added value back home.

Western Balkans–Visegrad Fellowship

The Visegrad Fellowship Programme supports short-term academic mobility between the Western Balkans and the Visegrad Group. It enables PhD candidates and doctoral holders to undertake research, teaching, or academic study at host institutions across the two regions, fostering knowledge exchange and regional cooperation.

Capacity Building

Academies, training, and peer-learning that equip emerging leaders with the skills, knowledge, and networks to drive cooperation and reconciliation.

WBF Regional Academy

A flagship capacity-building programme of the Western Balkans Fund, delivered through online learning and a seven-day residential academy. Supported by the Open Society Foundation – Western Balkans, it equips junior professionals and emerging leaders with the knowledge, skills, and regional networks to advance dialogue, cooperation, and reconciliation across the Western Balkans. This year's edition is on Dialogue and Culture of Cooperation.
