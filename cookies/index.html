Cookie Policy

What cookies this website uses and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember user preferences, improve performance, and provide anonymous information about website usage.

How We Use Cookies

The Western Balkans Fund uses cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Improve website performance and reliability
  • Remember user preferences where applicable
  • Analyze website traffic and visitor behaviour
  • Enhance security and detect technical issues

Where required by law, visitors will be asked to consent to the use of non-essential cookies.

Types of Cookies

Our website may use:

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the operation and security of the website and cannot be disabled without affecting core functionality.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with the website so that we can improve content, navigation, and performance. Information collected is generally aggregated and does not directly identify individual users.

Preference Cookies

These remember user preferences, such as language or display settings, to provide a more personalized browsing experience.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to manage or disable cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of the website.
