Regional Academy images
========================

Place the logos and photos used by regional-academy/index.html in this folder.

Partner-strip logos (referenced by the page):
  - wbf-logo.png   -> Western Balkans Fund logo
  - osf-logo.png   -> Open Society Foundation Western Balkans logo

You can drop the files straight in here with those exact names and they will
appear in the partner strip. Transparent PNG or SVG both work; if you use SVG,
save them as wbf-logo.svg / osf-logo.svg and update the <img src> in the page.


Photos of the 2026 edition
--------------------------

Two blocks on the page find their photos on their own. In both cases just name
the files 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg and so on, with no gaps in the numbering: the page
keeps looking for the next number and stops at the first one that is missing.
.jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work. Until a folder has photos in it, the page
shows a quiet dashed placeholder instead.

  2026/highlights/   The collage right under the hero. It is a mosaic: the
                     first photo runs large, so put the strongest one first.
                     Six photos fill it exactly; more simply continue below.

  2026/              The gallery inside "The 2026 edition" at the foot of the
                     page. Any number of photos, all the same size.

Please resize before adding them: about 1600px on the long side, saved as JPEG,
is plenty for the web and keeps the page fast.
