Regional Academy images
========================

Place the logos and photos used by html/regional-academy.html in this folder.

Partner-strip logos (referenced by the page):
  - wbf-logo.png   -> Western Balkans Fund logo
  - osf-logo.png   -> Open Society Foundation Western Balkans logo

You can drop the files straight in here with those exact names and they will
appear in the partner strip. Transparent PNG or SVG both work; if you use SVG,
save them as wbf-logo.svg / osf-logo.svg and update the <img src> in the page.
