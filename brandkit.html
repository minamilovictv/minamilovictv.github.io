03 — Typography

Typography

06 — Usage

Do & don't

✓ Do

  • Lead with navy; use teal, amber and blue as sparing accents.
  • Set all type in Inter with the defined scale and weights.
  • Give the emblem clear space equal to one dot on all sides.
  • Use navy-tinted shadows for elevation — never neutral grey.
  • Say "Contracting Parties" / "CP", never "countries".

✕ Don't

  • Recolour the emblem outside navy, white or a single mono tone.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add effects (drop-shadow, outline) to the mark.
  • Fill large areas with accent colours or use them for body text.
  • Swap in a different typeface or use light weights for headings.
  • Place the logo on a busy image without a navy scrim.
