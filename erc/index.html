About the 2nd Edition

Larger regional projects, led by
established civil society

Empowering Regional Cooperation (ERC) is the Western Balkans Fund’s larger-scale regional grant scheme. The 2nd Edition is implemented under Work Package 1 of the EU/WBF Joint Action, Phase III, and builds on the ERC 1st Edition piloted in 2024.

ERC funded projects should demonstrate potential for regional policy influence rather than focusing solely on local outputs. Proposals are expected to incorporate innovative approaches and provide evidence of scalable or transferable impact, and are assessed on their ability to contribute to broader regional policy, advocacy, knowledge or best practice sharing.

The overall objective is to enhance the role and contribution of civil society organisations in advancing regional cooperation and the EU integration process in the Western Balkans, through stronger organisational capacities, cooperation and wider recognition of their impact.

Contribution to EU integration

Every ERC project must show how it advances EU-aligned reforms, good-neighbourly relations and people-to-people links. EU Integration is a mandatory cross-cutting issue, scored under this call.

Areas of Intervention

Three areas to work in

Proposals must fall within one of these areas, or a combination of them. Activities under each should act as strategic accelerators for regional cooperation, institutional innovation and EU integration.

01

Cultural cooperation

Supporting organisations that empower, advocate and influence policy decisions related to cultural regional cooperation.

Indicative activities
  • Capacity building in cultural cooperation, cultural management and audience development
  • Regional peer learning and exchange of European experience
  • Advocacy for cultural diversity, intercultural dialogue and reconciliation
  • Partnerships among CSOs, cultural institutions and other stakeholders
  • Dialogue on the role of civil society and culture in EU integration
02

Education and scientific exchanges

Supporting organisations in education, science and the importance of active participation in democracy, through civic education programmes, workshops and public awareness campaigns.

Indicative activities
  • Partnerships between CSOs, educational and research institutions
  • Capacity building in inclusive education, lifelong learning and research cooperation
  • Advocacy for civil society participation in education, research and innovation
  • Peer learning among CSOs, educators and researchers
03

Sustainable development

Supporting organisations in inclusive multi-stakeholder partnerships advocating for policies and developing regional initiatives that are key to social progress and reflect the economic, environmental and social sides of sustainable development.

Indicative activities
  • Peer learning on shared sustainable development challenges
  • Awareness raising on climate resilience and European sustainability principles
  • Networks of CSOs, experts and communities around environmental priorities
  • Capacity building in climate action, green transition and circular economy
  • Dialogue on the Green Agenda for the Western Balkans
Who takes part

Target groups

All non-State, non-profit organisations through which people organise to pursue shared cultural, educational, social or economic objectives.

Who benefits

Final beneficiaries

Communities across the WB6, who gain from improved neighbourly relations and regional cooperation on the path towards EU integration.

Eligibility

Who can apply

ERC is open to non-profit organisations from the WBF Contracting Parties, applying together as a regional partnership.

The lead applicant must be

  • A legal entity (individuals are not eligible)
  • Established in one of the WBF Contracting Parties
  • Non-profit making
  • Able to show ongoing experience implementing donor-funded regional projects for at least the last three years, through experience cards in the online application form

Project partners must be organisations of the same kind. Organisations without three years of regional project experience cannot lead an ERC project but can join one as partners. To lead a regional project, they should consider the Grants for Grassroots Initiatives (GGI) call instead.

Who cannot apply. Profit-making companies, political parties, organisations dealing mostly or only with political or religious activities, and central government entities are not eligible under this call, neither as Lead Applicant nor as Project Partner. The same applies to central and local government entities, units and directorates, and to government-funded organisations such as museums and universities, which may take part only as associated institutions. An application that does not meet all of these requirements will not be eligible.

Partnership is required

Every ERC project is delivered by a regional partnership of eligible organisations, working together on shared regional challenges.

1 Lead Applicant + at least 3 Project Partners
Find partnerson the WBF Partnership Platform
  • The partnership includes organisations from at least four different WBF Contracting Parties. Partnerships spanning more than four are encouraged and may be prioritised.
  • The lead applicant submits the proposal, signs the Declaration by the Lead Applicant and the grant agreement with WBF, and represents the partnership.
  • The lead applicant must co-finance the project. It may share expenditure with partners, but as contract signatory it remains solely responsible for implementation, spending and reporting.
  • An organisation may apply several times, as lead or as partner, but can be awarded only one grant as Lead Applicant and one as Project Partner (the highest scored).
Associated institutions. State agencies, ministries, museums, universities and other public institutions can take part as associated institutions, outside the partnership. They may help carry out activities but must have no financial or other interest in the project beyond travel and subsistence directly linked to its activities. They do not sign the Partnership Agreement, and their role is specified in the application form, though that is not required if you have not identified one. Including them is optional and is not part of the eligibility criteria.

WBF Financial Regulation

Financial principles

  • Co-financing is required from the applicant, and may not be made in kind.
  • Double financing is prohibited; costs already financed by another EU-funded action are not eligible.
  • Grants must not produce a profit for beneficiaries.

Funds are distributed in a balanced way across the Contracting Parties. WBF may not award all funds, or may cancel the call, if proposals are insufficient in quality or number.

HR

Evaluation

How applications are assessed

WBF, as Contracting Authority, examines and evaluates applications with the help of external assessors. The list of recommended projects is endorsed by the WBF Governing Bodies, and funds are distributed in a balanced way across the Contracting Parties.

  1. 1
    Step 1

    Administrative and eligibility checks

    The deadline, eligibility, partnership, format and documents are checked. An application that fails is rejected on that basis alone.

  2. 2
    Step 2

    Evaluation of applications

    Applications that pass are evaluated on the relevance and design of the proposed action.

  3. 3
    Step 3

    Scoring of full applications

    Each application is scored out of 100 against the grid below. Applications below 55 points are not considered further.

  4. 4
    Step 4

    Supporting documents

    Shortlisted applicants upload supporting documents through OGMS, so WBF can verify the eligibility of the partnership.

Before scoring: eligibility checklist

Submission
  • The application arrived before the deadline
  • The application was submitted through OGMS
  • The application is typed and written in English
  • The Declaration by the Lead Applicant is completed, signed and stamped
  • No requested information or document is missing or incorrect
Applicants and partnership
  • The lead applicant and partners are non-profit legal entities established in the WB6
  • There is 1 Lead Applicant and at least 3 Project Partners from four different WB6 Contracting Parties
Project and budget
  • The proposal meets the eligibility criteria for funding, duration, applicants, partnership, costs and activities
  • The project lasts between 6 and 10 months, including the 30-day reporting period
  • The requested contribution is between €20,000 and €25,000
  • The budget is completed in the requested format, in EUR
