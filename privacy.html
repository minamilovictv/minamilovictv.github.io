Privacy Policy

How the Western Balkans Fund handles personal data across this website, our application platform, and our communications.

Introduction

The Western Balkans Fund ("WBF", "the Fund", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, online application platform, and related digital services. We process personal data responsibly, fairly, and transparently, in accordance with applicable data protection laws and internationally recognized privacy principles.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, how it is protected, and the rights available to individuals whose personal data we process.

Who We Are

The Western Balkans Fund is an international organization established by the Governments of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo*, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Serbia to promote regional cooperation, good neighbourly relations, and sustainable development throughout the Western Balkans.

The WBF Secretariat, based in Tirana, Albania, acts as the controller of personal data collected through this website.

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact:

Email: info@westernbalkansfund.org

Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with the Fund, we may collect:

  • Name and surname
  • Organization or institution
  • Professional title
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Country of residence
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Grant application and project documentation
  • Event registration details
  • Newsletter subscription information
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device information, pages visited, and website usage statistics

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and fulfill our institutional responsibilities.

How We Use Your Information

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries and correspondence
  • Managing grant applications and project implementation
  • Organizing events, trainings, workshops, and public activities
  • Communicating with applicants, beneficiaries, partners, and stakeholders
  • Administering newsletter subscriptions
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Producing anonymous statistical analyses
  • Maintaining website security
  • Complying with financial, legal, contractual, and audit obligations

Personal data is processed only for legitimate and clearly defined purposes.

Sharing Information

Personal information may be shared only where necessary with:

  • Authorized members of the WBF Secretariat
  • External evaluators and experts
  • Donor institutions and funding partners where required
  • Service providers supporting website hosting or information technology
  • Independent auditors
  • Competent public authorities where disclosure is required by law

The Western Balkans Fund does not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with legal, financial, contractual, and archival obligations. When no longer required, information is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

Where applicable, individuals may request to:

  • Access their personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw previously given consent
  • Receive a copy of their personal data where legally applicable

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided above.

Security

The Fund implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse. Although every reasonable measure is taken to protect information, no online system can guarantee absolute security.
