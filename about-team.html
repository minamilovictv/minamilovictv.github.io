Our Team

The Western Balkans Fund Secretariat, headquartered in Tirana, Albania, is a geographically balanced team of professionals representing all six Contracting Parties. Bringing together diverse expertise in programme management, grant administration, finance, communications, and institutional development, the Secretariat works closely with partners, beneficiaries, and institutions across the region. United by a shared commitment to regional cooperation, the team ensures the effective implementation of the Fund's initiatives while promoting dialogue, mutual trust, and lasting connections throughout the Western Balkans.
